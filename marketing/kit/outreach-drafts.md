# Outreach drafts (not sent)

Drafts only. Nothing here has been sent. Before you send anything, check it against
these rules:

- Ask for one thing only: try the sample brief, or book a 15-minute walkthrough.
- Never ask an owner to send you their sales file. They open their own export in
  Tally on their own device.
- No prices, results, customer counts, testimonials or "AI" claims. Tally shows what
  changed. It doesn't prove why.
- The privacy line comes from the app: "No account needed to import a file. The file
  is processed in your browser." Keep Square wording separate (read-only connection).
- Chinese lines are marked ZH-REVIEW. Have a fluent reader check them first.

Fill in the `[brackets]`. The link is https://bus-idea.pages.dev (add `?ref=email-a`
or `?ref=wechat-a` so visits show up under that tag on the Founder page).

---

## 1. Email, English

**Subject:** One thing to try at [Shop name] next week

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your name], a Brandeis student building Tally, a weekly sales check-in for

https://bus-idea.pages.dev/?ref=email-a

No account needed to import a file. The file is processed in your browser. Please
don't email me your sales file. If you'd like, I can sit with you for 15 minutes
while you open your own export in Tally.

Would you take a look at the sample this week?

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone or email]
