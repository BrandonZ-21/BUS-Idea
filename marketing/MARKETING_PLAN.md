# Tally marketing plan

Window: Tue Oct 6 to Tue Oct 20, 2026 (two weeks). Budget: $0. Time: a few hours a week. Starting point: one outside user.

Drafts only. You approve and send everything yourself.

## What this plan is for

Two weeks is not enough to "grow" anything. The goal is to learn, from 2 to 3 more real owners, whether Tally is worth an owner's time when Square already gives them a free AI assistant.

Targets (not promises):

- 8 to 10 businesses approached
- 3 conversations with an owner or manager
- 1 or 2 owners who put their own data in while you watch

If you end with one more real user and three honest "no, because..." answers, that is a good result for this course.

## Positioning

"Insights in plain English" is not a differentiator: Square AI and Toast IQ already do that. Each line below is a hypothesis to test, not a claim.

| Target group | One-sentence positioning |
|---|---|
| Square cafes and bakeries near Moody St and Main St | A free one-page report of your busy hours and top items from your Square sales, with nothing to type or ask, and a student who will sit with you while you try it. |
| Chinese-speaking owners on Square | The same report with an English/Chinese toggle, and someone who can walk you through it in Chinese in person. |
| Other Waltham food businesses on Square | Connect Square read-only or upload a sales file, and see your sales by hour and day and your top items in a few minutes, saved only in your browser. |

What you can honestly say is different today: a fixed report (no prompting), the English/Chinese toggle, sales saved only in the owner's browser, and in-person help. Item-name merging and a local calendar are not built; ask about them as problems, do not offer them.

## Target segments, ranked

1. **Independent cafes and bakeries on Square, walking distance (Moody St, Main St).** Best fit with what your one user liked: one-click connect, the heatmap, the items view. The owner or manager is often on site. Includes caffe ollo and Peipei's Bakery, if their registers are Square.
2. **Chinese-speaking owners of food businesses on Square.** Smaller group and unconfirmed, but it is where you have something Square AI may not offer, and you can speak with them in person. Blocked on two things in READINESS_CHECKLIST.md (#6, #7).
3. **Other Waltham food businesses on Square** (restaurants, counters, Lexington St, including Banh Mi Oi). Same product, but full-service restaurants care more about labour and food cost, which Tally does not cover.

Not a fit: businesses on Toast or another register. Check at the counter before you pitch: a Square reader or terminal is visible to any customer.

Do not post in Square's own Community forum.

### Decision rule for Oct 13

Fewer than 2 conversations done or booked? Do one of these, not both:

- A second walk-in round to 5 new Square businesses (preferred: it keeps your in-person advantage), or
- One Facebook group post, after reading the group's rules and asking an admin.

## What not to do

- Do not use or mention any business's real sales numbers, including your workplace's, in any message, flyer, screenshot or post. Demo with the built-in sample data only.
- Do not say "plugin", "security-approved", "AI" or "used by local businesses".
- Do not promise results. The app's own footer says its suggestions are "not guarantees".
- Do not pitch during a rush. If there is a line, leave the flyer and come back.
