## Wording you can reuse safely

These lines match the Privacy page and the code. Copy them; do not paraphrase them into something stronger.

- "Tally is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Square."
- "Tally asks Square for read-only access: your business name and locations, and your completed orders. It can never charge, refund or change anything in Square."
- "Your sales are saved only in your browser on your device. Tally's server does not store them."
- "Tally's server stores your Square merchant ID and business name, Square's access keys (encrypted), when you last synced, and your sign-in sessions."
- "You can disconnect and delete your account from the My Data page, or remove Tally from the Apps section of your Square Dashboard, at any time."
- "Tally counts anonymous steps, like 'opened Tally' or 'connected Square', to find confusing spots. It never includes your sales, item names, amounts or business name."
- "Totals are gross sales, before discounts and refunds, so they can read a little higher than your Square Dashboard."

Do not write: "100% private", "no tracking", "your data never leaves your device" (Square sales pass through Tally's server on the way to the browser), "Tally never sees customer details", "plugin", "security-approved", "AI-powered", "trusted by".

## Longer email

Subject: Would 15 minutes of feedback be possible? (Brandeis student project)

Hi [Name or "there"],

I'm Brandon, a student at Brandeis. For a course on testing startup ideas, I built a small web app called Tally for cafes, bakeries and restaurants that use Square. I'd be grateful for 15 minutes of your honest opinion.

What it does: you connect Square (read-only) or upload a sales export, and it shows one fixed report. Two honest limits: totals are gross sales, before discounts and refunds, so they can read a little higher than your Square Dashboard. And the first sync covers the last 90 days.

I know Square has its own AI assistant in the Dashboard. Part of what I'm trying to learn is whether a simple fixed report is useful next to that, or not. "Not useful" is a helpful answer for me.

If you're open to it, I can stop by at a quiet time, [day] between 2 and 4, and sit with you while you try it. Or you can try it yourself at https://bus-idea.pages.dev (there's sample data if you'd rather not connect anything).

Brandon Zhao

## 30-second walk-in script

Go mid-afternoon. If there's a line, leave the flyer and come back. Ask for the owner or manager first.

> "Hi, I'm Brandon, a Brandeis student. I'm not selling anything. For a class I built a free web page that turns Square sales into a one-page report: busy hours, top items, that kind of thing. I noticed you use Square. I'm looking for a few local owners to try it for 15 minutes and tell me if it's actually useful or not. It's read-only, and it's my own project, not part of Square. Is there a quiet time this week I could come back and show you?"

If they say yes now: open the sample data first, then let them decide whether to connect.

If the owner is out: "Could I leave this for them?" Leave the flyer, write the date on it, and ask for the best time to come back.

Before they connect, say three things: it's read-only; totals are gross, before discounts and refunds; and "since only a few people are testing, I can see anonymous counts of steps like 'connected Square', so I'll be able to tell roughly whether it worked for you. I can't see your sales."
