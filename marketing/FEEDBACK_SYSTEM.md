# Tally feedback system

Four parts: a tracking log, interview questions, a say-versus-did check against the Founder dashboard, and a themes table.

Keep this file private. It names businesses and what they told you. Never copy a business's sales figures into it: record what the owner said and did, not their numbers.

## 1. Tracking log

One row per business. Update it the same day.

Status: `not contacted` → `contacted` → `replied` → `session booked` → `tried it` → `interviewed`, or `declined` / `no reply` / `not a fit`.

Rules:

- Public business contact details only. No personal phone numbers or private emails.
- "Square at register?" is what you saw at the counter, not a guess.
- Record a "no" and its reason. A clear "no, because Square already does this" is data.

## 2. Interview questions

Ask after they have tried it, not before. Ask, then stay quiet. Write their words, not your summary.

1. Before today, how did you check how the business was doing? How often?
2. Have you tried the AI assistant in your Square Dashboard? What happened?
3. What did you expect to see when the report opened? What was missing?
4. Which part would you actually look at again? Which part would you never open?
5. Do any of your item names split one product into several (sizes, flavours, old and new names)? How do you deal with that today?
6. What would you want to know before connecting your Square account to a tool like this?
7. Would you use this weekly? What day and time would you open it, and what would remind you?

If you have 30 more seconds: "Was there a number you didn't believe?"

Avoid "Do you like it?" and "Would you pay for it?". People are polite to students.

If these questions also feed your course handout's founder questions, put those in your own words.

## 3. What they said versus what they did

The Founder dashboard (`#/founder`, Live data) shows anonymous step counts by browser. It cannot tell you who. Use it like this:

1. **One owner at a time.** Note the Live numbers before a session and again after. With this few users, the change is that owner.
2. **Tell them.** Before they connect: "Since only a few people are testing, I can see anonymous counts of steps like 'connected Square', so I'll be able to tell roughly whether it worked for you. I can't see your sales."
3. **Check back once, a week later**, for "Came back with new data".
4. **Never open the database to look up an owner.** The Privacy page says you don't.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| | "I saved it to look at later" | 3. Accounts that saved at least one report | | |
| | "The upload didn't work" | 5. Recent failures (reason and time) | | |

What the dashboard cannot tell you:

- **Which pages they opened.** There is one "saw a report" step, nothing per page. "I liked the heatmap" can only be checked by watching.
- **Anything from a browser that sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.** Those are not counted at all. Zero does not prove they did not come back.
- **People.** It counts browsers. One owner on a phone and a laptop is two.
- **Your own browser** is excluded once you open the Founder page in it. If you demo on your own laptop, that session will not appear. Have the owner use their own device when the count matters.

When said and did disagree, believe did, and ask about the gap: "You mentioned you'd check it weekly. What got in the way?"
