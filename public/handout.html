TALLY · WEEKLY BRIEF

Bring one sales export.
Leave with one thing to try.

带一份销售导出文件，
带走一件下周可以试的事。

Tally turns your register's sales export into a short weekly brief for takeout shops and cafés, in English and Chinese.

Tally 把收银机导出的销售数据变成一份简短的每周简报，中英文都可以看。

  1. Spot 发现

    One change from last week, with the numbers beside it.

    和上周相比的一个变化，附上数字。

  2. Try 尝试

    One small thing to try next week.

    下周可以试的一件小事。

  3. Check 检查

    The number to look at a week later.

    一周后要看的那个数字。

No app link is set. Add appUrl in js/config.js, then reload.

Scan to try the sample brief

扫码看看示例简报

No account needed to import a file. The file is processed in your browser.

导入文件不需要注册账号。文件在你的浏览器里处理。

Please don't email or text your sales file to anyone. Open it in Tally yourself.

请不要把销售文件发给任何人，自己在 Tally 里打开就好。

Using Square? You can connect it instead. Connecting Square is read-only: Tally can never charge, refund or change anything in Square.

使用 Square？也可以直接连接。连接 Square 是只读的：Tally 不能收款、退款或修改 Square 里的任何内容。
