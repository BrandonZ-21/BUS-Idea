## Executive brief

Tally has one outside user (the founder's manager), a class-only goal ending early to mid December, 1-2 hours a week, $0 and no outside accounts or contacts (user-confirmed 2026-11-02). So this is a **six-week learning pilot**, not a growth plan.

Research on Square's own seller forum (27 items, 2017-2026) shows a long-standing request Square never built into its standard reports: **average sales by weekday and by hour across many weeks.** Owners rebuild it in spreadsheets, or keep weather and event notes on paper. Tally already does both. Square's free AI tools (Square AI, Managerbot since April 2026) can answer such questions if asked, which is the main competitive risk. Some owners say they avoid AI or find the bot noisy.

**Recommendation:** walk into independent Square cafes and bakeries on Moody St and Main St, Waltham, plus one introduction from the manager, with one specific promise: *"See your average Tuesday, hour by hour, from your Square sales. Nothing to type or ask."* Goal: 3 owners see their own data in a 15-minute sit-down; learn whether anyone comes back unprompted within a week.
