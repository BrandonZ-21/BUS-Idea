## Weekly rhythm (Fridays, 30 minutes)

| Item | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Fill the scorecard row (PILOT_AND_FEEDBACK.md) | 10 |
| Founder page: sources, own-data reports, returns, useful yes/no, failures | 5 |
| Triage new feedback into opportunity cards | 5 |
| Listening: field guide rows 1-3 (RESEARCH.md §5) | 10 |

Weekly total including the walk-in round or sit-downs: about 1.5 h. If only 1 h is free: skip listening, then the walk-in round; never skip follow-ups with owners you've met.

**Weekly review questions:** What did we do? What happened? What did owners say, and what did they do? Where did it break down? Which explanation has the most support? What do we keep, change, stop or test next?
