# Tally brand voice

Version 1.0 (proposed), 2026-11-02. Status: **proposed** until the founder adopts it. Built from the existing outreach drafts ([marketing/OUTREACH_KIT.md](../../marketing/OUTREACH_KIT.md)), the in-app wording ([public/js/translations.js](../../public/js/translations.js), [public/js/advice-text.js](../../public/js/advice-text.js)), and the strategy ([STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md)).

## Who we sound like

When someone reads our words, it should feel like talking to **a careful student who has looked at a lot of sales data and is a little nervous about wasting your time.** They come to your counter at a quiet moment, say who they are before anything else, show you one thing that's actually about your shop, tell you what they don't know, and leave when you're busy.

- **Relationship to the customer:** a helper, not a vendor. Peer-level respect for someone who runs a business.
- **Conversation style:** answers first, then the reason. Short sentences. Asks one question at a time.
- **Explaining:** uses the owner's own numbers and plain words ("your average Tuesday", not "temporal distribution").
- **Confidence:** states what the data shows directly; says how many days a number is based on.
- **Disagreement:** "Square might already show you this. If it does, tell me." No defensiveness.
- **Uncertainty:** "Not enough data yet" instead of a guess. "This is a comparison, not proof."
- **Under pressure (a mistake, an angry owner):** owns it, says what happened, says what will happen next, and only promises what's verified.

## Defining traits

| Trait | Observable writing behavior | Approved | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Specific** | Names the day, hour, item and number of days | "Your Tuesdays from 2 to 4 PM average $41, across 9 Tuesdays." *(illustrative numbers)* | "Unlock powerful insights into your business performance." |
| **Honest about limits** | Says what a number includes and what we can't know | "Gross sales, before discounts and refunds." | "Your true revenue at a glance." |
| **Low-pressure** | Offers a sample first; one clear next step; easy to say no | "Want to see the sample first? Then decide." | "Don't miss out, connect now!" |
| **Plain** | Everyday words; jargon explained once | "Heatmap: darker squares are busier hours." | "Leverage our analytics dashboard." |
| **Respectful of time** | Short; says how long things take | "Fifteen minutes, at a quiet time." | Long paragraphs before the point |

## Mandatory writing rules

**We MUST:**
- MUST begin with useful information, a situation the owner recognizes, or their outcome.
- MUST name concrete tasks and outcomes ("staff Tuesday afternoons", "prep fewer muffins").
- MUST say what a number includes (gross/net, date range, number of days) when we show one.
- MUST disclose who we are early: a Brandeis student project, not affiliated with Square, free.
- MUST use familiar words and explain any necessary term in one line.
- MUST write sentences a person could say out loud at a counter; use contractions.
- MUST label estimates and illustrations as such ("an illustration, not a prediction").
- MUST give one clear next action and say what happens after it.
- MUST respect "no" and silence: one follow-up at most.
- MUST keep the English and Chinese versions saying the same facts.

**We MUST NOT:**
- MUST NOT describe Tally as "AI" or "AI-powered" (contrasting is fine: "nothing to ask an AI"), or say "plugin", "security-approved", "trusted by", "used by local businesses", "100% private", "no tracking", or "your data never leaves your device".
- MUST NOT promise results ("increase sales", "boost profits") or claim a change *caused* a result.
- MUST NOT say "profit" when we mean sales.
- MUST NOT invent testimonials, quotes, numbers, users or feelings. Synthetic examples are labeled.
- MUST NOT show or mention any real business's sales numbers in public material, including the founder's workplace.
- MUST NOT manufacture urgency, scarcity or shame ("your competitors already know…").
- MUST NOT joke about a slow day, a mistake, or an owner's difficulty.
- MUST NOT ask for feedback we have no plan to read and act on.

## Style specifics

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pronouns | "you/your" for the owner; "I" for the founder in person and email; "Tally" or "we" in the app |
| Formality | Conversational, polite; no slang |
| Technical depth | Owner-level; percentages and averages yes, statistics terms no |
| Rhythm | Mostly short sentences; vary length naturally |
| Headlines | Sentence case ("Your average Tuesday, hour by hour") |
| Punctuation | Plain; no exclamation marks in product copy; avoid em dashes |
| Humor | Light and rare, never about the owner's business |
| Emoji | None in outreach and product copy |
| Metaphors | Avoid ("hidden gold in your data") |
| Calls to action | Verb + what happens: "See the sample", "Connect Square (read-only)", "Book 15 minutes" |
| Chinese | Simple Mandarin, polite 您 in first contact, 你 in the app; every new Chinese line reviewed by a fluent reader before printing ([ZH_REVIEW.md](../../ZH_REVIEW.md)) |

### Vocabulary

| Prefer | Context-dependent | Avoid (and use instead) |
|---|---|---|
| average Tuesday, busy hours, slow stretch | "insight" (in-app section names only) | "unlock insights" → "see your average day" |
| gross sales, before discounts and refunds | "forecast" (only for weather) | "true revenue" → "gross sales" |
| try this for 2 weeks | "AI" (only when contrasting: "nothing to ask an AI") | "AI-powered" → (cut) |
| read-only, saved in your browser | "data" | "secure" alone → say what is stored |
| a comparison, not proof | "free" (true today) | "guaranteed", "increase", "boost" |

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Walk-in / ad | Brief, curious, no pressure | "Quick question, not selling anything: do you know what a normal Tuesday afternoon looks like here?" |
| Landing page | Clear, specific | "Your average Tuesday, hour by hour." |
| Explanation | Patient, concrete | "Each square is one hour of one weekday. Darker means more sales on average." |
| Onboarding | Encouraging, practical | "Start with the sample. You can connect Square after." |
| Success | Plain, not gushing | "Done. Your report covers 74 selling days." *(illustrative)* |
| Feedback request | Optional, specific | "What would make this more useful next time? One line is plenty." |
| Support / confusion | Calm, step by step | "That total is gross, before discounts and refunds, so it can read higher than Square's net. Here's how to compare one day." |
| Complaint | Own it, no excuses | "You're right, that number was off. Here's what happened and what I changed." |
| Mistake | Responsibility + verified next step | See example below |
| Delay | Honest, no deadline guesses | "I haven't fixed it yet. I'll message you when it's done, not before." |
| Cancellation | Easy, grateful | "Disconnect is on the My Data page. Thanks for trying it." |

## Visual expression

**Observed (keep):** DM Sans (Google Fonts, SIL OFL); light gray background `#F6F7F9`, white cards, 12px radius; one signal color, amber `#F59E0B`, for "this matters"; blue `#2F6FED` for links and chrome; a lock "Private to you" badge ([public/css/style.css](../../public/css/style.css) lines 8-22). Charts use slate bars with amber highlights.

**Proposed:**
- **Imagery:** real screens of the sample report, never stock photos of baristas, never any real shop's numbers.
- **Composition for the 3-second hook:** one big phrase ("Your average Tuesday") + one heatmap image. For 10 seconds: a single line of how it works + the QR code.
- **Motif:** the weekday-by-hour heatmap is the recognizable Tally image; use it on the card and as the first thing shown on a phone.
- **Color mood:** calm and practical; amber only on the one thing to look at.

**Visual rules:** MUST use the sample data in every visual. MUST keep amber for one highlight per image. MUST show the bilingual line on printed pieces once reviewed. MUST NOT use Square's logo or colors in a way that suggests affiliation. MUST NOT imply these proposed visuals already exist; the card ([public/card.html](../../public/card.html)) is the only printed asset today, and it has no heatmap image yet.

## Tally brand voice: mandatory writing rules

*Version 1.0 (proposed), 2026-11-02. Paste this block into any writing task about Tally. It works without any other document.*

**Purpose:** help owners of small Square cafes and bakeries see their own sales patterns. **Audience:** busy owners and managers, English or Chinese. **Human portrait:** a careful student who looked at your sales, shows you one thing about your shop, says what they don't know, and leaves when you're busy.

**Behaviors:** specific (day, hour, number of days); honest about what numbers include; low-pressure (sample first, one next step); plain words; short.

**MUST:** start with something useful or recognizable · name concrete outcomes · say what a number includes (gross, date range, days) · disclose "Brandeis student project, not affiliated with Square, free" early · use words you'd say at a counter · label illustrations and estimates · give one clear next action · keep EN and ZH saying the same facts.

**MUST NOT:** say AI-powered, plugin, security-approved, trusted by, used by local businesses, 100% private, no tracking, "data never leaves your device" · promise results or claim causes · say profit for sales · invent quotes, numbers, users or feelings · show real businesses' numbers · create urgency or shame · joke about difficulties · ask for feedback you won't use.

**Vocabulary:** average Tuesday, busy hours, slow stretch, gross sales before discounts and refunds, try this for 2 weeks, a comparison not proof, read-only, saved in your browser.

**Tone boundaries:** curious at first contact; patient when explaining; plain about success; responsible and specific about mistakes; no deadlines you haven't verified.

**Examples:** Hook: "Do you know what a normal Tuesday afternoon looks like here?" · Headline: "Your average Tuesday, hour by hour" · CTA: "See the sample" · Mistake: say what happened, what changed, what the owner should do.

**Audit before sending:** Is it relevant to this owner? Could I say it aloud? Is there a concrete value? Is every claim true today (check the Privacy page and the code)? Does it avoid hype and pressure? Does it leave the owner in control? Are terms explained? Cut anything that does none of these. Fix violations before delivery.

*Changes to this core voice must be explicit and versioned. Channel-specific versions must not redefine it.*
